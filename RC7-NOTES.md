# Famme Journey Studio 2.0.0-rc.7

## Locked read-only viewer

RC7 hardens View mode after real-world testing. The viewer is now a presentation surface, not an editor with disabled buttons.

- Nodes cannot be dragged.
- The viewport cannot be panned or zoomed with mouse/touch gestures.
- Connections cannot be selected or reconnected.
- Selection rectangles and interaction artifacts are hidden.
- Funnel stage zones remain fixed relative to the journey.
- Nodes can still be clicked to inspect URL, tracking and creative details.
- Connection arrows continue to render because hidden handles remain measurable.
- The inspector opens as an overlay and does not shift the graph.

Print/PDF behavior from RC6 is unchanged.
